My story

Hello! I'm Niyonshuti Dick Jameson

I grew up in Rwanda fascinated by how things work — taking apart old radios, then old phones, and eventually discovering that I could build things myself with nothing more than a laptop and an idea.

That curiosity led me to study Software Engineering — and to start building real products for real clients. Today I'm the CEO of True Heart Systems and CEO of Trunova Systems LTD, where I lead teams that design, build and run web platforms end-to-end with React, Laravel and Java.

Professionally, I'm interested in building digital tools for education, agriculture and public services in Africa — products that are fast on low bandwidth, work beautifully on mobile, and are accessible to everyone.
